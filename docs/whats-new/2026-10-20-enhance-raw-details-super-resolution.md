### Enhance: Raw Details and Super Resolution
- Photo ▸ Enhance ▸ Raw Details demosaics a raw again with a slower edge-aware method (finer detail, less false colour and zipper) into a new DNG next to it, stacked with the original and given its edits.
- Photo ▸ Enhance ▸ Super Resolution does the same and doubles the width and height; it takes JPEG and TIFF photos too. Both run in the background, with their progress in the activity stack.
