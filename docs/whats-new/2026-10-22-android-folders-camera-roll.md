### Android: import folders and your camera roll

- File ▸ Import from Folder… works on Android: it was greyed out there. A folder you pick is imported with everything in it, folders inside included, and stays readable after the app restarts.
- On the first start the app offers to add your camera roll (DCIM/Camera). It asks Android for access to your photos only, not to all files. If you say Not Now, Settings ▸ Folders ▸ Camera roll ▸ Import… offers it again.
