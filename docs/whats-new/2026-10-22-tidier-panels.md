### Tidier panels and menus

- The tool strip at the far right is gone: show / hide the right panel from the top bar; Library's Versions panel is in its left column, as in Develop; About stays in the Help menu. The Activity bell is gone from the top bar.
- The histogram is taller, and a second histogram can show under it (right-click the histogram ▸ Second Histogram), e.g. RGB Overlay above RGB Parade.
- The photo's right-click menu groups its less-used items into submenus, so it fits on a 1280 × 720 screen.
- Narrower slider names and margins in Develop's right panel and Library's right column; HDR Headroom is now Headroom.
- The Web module loads thumbnails of photos not yet seen in the Library.
- Settings ▸ Performance ▸ HDR display turns the HDR window off (next launch), for screens where the desktop blurs or stretches small text in it.
