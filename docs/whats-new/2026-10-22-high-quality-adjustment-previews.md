### High quality adjustment previews
- Turn on High quality adjustment previews under Settings ▸ Performance to keep the image as sharp while you move a slider as it is when you let go.
- When an edit is too slow for that (many masks, a large display, no GPU), the image updates faster while the slider moves and sharpens as soon as you hold it still, without letting go. The app remembers which sliders were slow on which photo.
