### Eboda Colour and Craft Colour

- **Eboda Colour** is a new Basic profile: with a camera profile (`.dcp`) such as Adobe Standard, it renders the brighter, more contrasty and more colourful look Lightroom Classic gives such photos by default.
- The base profile, Color, is now called **Craft Colour**: the app's own rendering. Existing edits look the same.
- A camera profile (`.dcp`) now keeps its own tone, or the default tone, instead of the camera's JPEG tone, which had made colours duller than the profile intends.
