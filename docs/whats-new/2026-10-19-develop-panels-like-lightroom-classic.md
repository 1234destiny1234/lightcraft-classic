### Develop panels in Lightroom Classic's order
- The Edit panel lists Lightroom Classic's Develop panels, in its order: Basic, Tone Curve, Color Mixer, Color Grading,
  Detail, Lens Corrections, Transform, Effects and Calibration. Tone Curve and Color Grading are panels of their own
  instead of rows that open inside Basic and Color, and Color Mixer has Mixer and Point Color tabs.
- Tone Curve, Color Mixer and Color Grading each have their own on/off switch. Basic has none, as in Lightroom
  Classic; an edit that has Basic or the old Color group switched off still shows the switch, to turn it back on.
- ⌘1–⌘9 open the panels top to bottom, as in Lightroom Classic.
