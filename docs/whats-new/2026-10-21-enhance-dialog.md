### The Enhance dialog
- Photo ▸ Enhance… (Ctrl+Alt+I, ⌘⌥I on macOS) opens Enhance Preview: choose Raw Details or Super Resolution and see the middle of the photo before and after; click the preview to zoom in, drag to pan.
- It shows the size of the new photo and an estimate of how long it takes, measured on your photo, and Create Stack keeps the new DNG stacked with the original.
