### Scopes that read like a grading suite

- The histogram, waveform, RGB parade and vectorscope now sit on a near-black well with an amber graticule, and their traces glow brighter where more of the photo sits.
- The Waveform now overlays the red, green and blue channels (white where they agree) instead of a grey luminance trace.
- The vectorscope gets degree ticks around its rim and bracketed colour targets.
