## Performance

- **Canon CR3 decodes its streams in parallel:** every tile's planes, and every subband of a C-RAW plane, are separate
  entropy streams and now decode together (they were decoded a tile at a time), and the samples are placed in the
  image a row at a time in parallel. The same samples: 35 corpus CR3s from 26 bodies decode 6.0 → 3.8 s in all;
  multi-tile C-RAW (EOS 90D, M6 Mark II) 264 → 91 ms. Thanks @Kaan88. (no PR)
