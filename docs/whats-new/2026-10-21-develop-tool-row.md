### Develop's tools under the histogram
- Crop, Remove, Red Eye and Masking are a row under the histogram, as Lightroom Classic's tool strip. Each tool's
  drawer opens above the Develop panels, which stay under it; a second click on the tool, its key, Return or the
  drawer's Close puts it away.
- The strip at the right edge keeps the button that folds the panels away, and Presets; a second click on Presets
  goes back to the tools.
