### Sliders stay under the pointer
- A slider's thumb now stays under the pointer while you drag it, as in Lightroom Classic. Dragging past an end leaves
  it there until the pointer comes back over it, and a slow drag no longer falls behind the pointer. Shift-drag still
  makes fine changes.
- Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Slider control chooses where pointer acceleration applies: Absolute, Acceleration on Slider
  (the earlier behaviour), Acceleration on Number (the default: scrubbing the value text accelerates) or Acceleration
  (both).
