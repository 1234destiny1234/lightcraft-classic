### People Mode that follows the view
- The **Named People** cards now count the photos the library shows: pick an album, a folder, Picks or a search in the filter bar, and only the people in those photos are listed (issue #685).
- The **Unnamed faces** list and a person's **More** faces follow the same photos, so a queue no longer offers faces from the rest of the library.
- A person's own faces stay the measure of who they look like, so a narrowed view still finds their look-alikes among the photos it shows.
- A card or a queue that is empty because of the view says so (choose All Photos, or clear the filter, to look everywhere), instead of suggesting the face is gone.
