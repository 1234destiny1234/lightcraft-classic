### Cleaner develop sliders

- Slider names no longer get cut off: names and values use a slightly smaller font with more room for the name, and sliders inside a titled group use short names (Hue and Saturation under Red Primary, Luminance and Color under Noise Reduction, Shadows / Midtones / Highlights under the grading wheels).
- A smaller, solid slider thumb and smaller group headings make the panel calmer.
- Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Sliders: choose One Row (name, track and value on a line) or Two Rows (name and value above a full-width track).
- Slow drags move a slider at 30 % speed, rising to full speed for quick ones, so fine changes are easier; Shift still gives a finer drag.
- Optics: the default for new photos is one quiet line under the lens controls.
