### Nikon Z6III raws no longer turn purple
- The Z6III calibration added on 2026-10-26 turned blue skies and grey surfaces purple: its hue/saturation table pushed
  blues toward violet and raised the saturation of near-greys up to eight times. It is removed until it can be refitted,
  so Z6III raws open with the built-in colour again, which matches the camera's own JPEG. A photo that had the
  calibrated look chosen by hand falls back to the camera default.
