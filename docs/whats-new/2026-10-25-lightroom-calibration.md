### Camera calibration matched to Lightroom
- New `calibrate-lightroom` command in the command-line tool: fit a camera's colour and profile looks (Adobe Standard, Adobe Color, …) to Lightroom's exports of your own raws, and send the small file of numbers it writes so every user of that camera gets matched colour.
- The file holds aggregate numbers only: no images, file names, dates, serial numbers, locations or lenses. The guide is docs/calibrate-lightroom.md.
