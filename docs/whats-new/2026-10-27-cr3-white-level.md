### Canon CR3 highlights keep their colour
- Canon CR3 raws whose bright areas (a backlit sky, sunlit clouds) were just under the sensor's limit no longer
  show a hard-edged pale band or ring where the brightness crosses the level Canon's file states: the EOS RP states
  a clipping point about 0.2 EV below where its sensor really clips, the EOS R100 at ISO 640 about 0.4 EV. That range
  is now kept as real light, so Highlights can bring it back. Everything darker renders as before.
