## Presets

- **Metadata and export presets import:** Lightroom Classic metadata and export presets (`.lrtemplate`), XMP metadata templates and the app's own `.lcmeta` / `.lcexport` files import as reusable presets; what can't be carried over is named.
- **Creator contact details:** job title, address, phone, email and website are stored, edited in the Metadata panel, kept in metadata presets and written to sidecars and exported files (new Copyright & Contact export metadata option).
- **Export presets** keep the destination folder and can be renamed, exported and imported; metadata presets can be renamed and exported.
