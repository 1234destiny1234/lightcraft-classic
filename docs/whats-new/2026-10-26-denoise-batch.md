### AI Denoise for many photos at once

- With Auto Sync on, the AI Denoise switch turns it on or off for every selected raw photo, in
  one undo step.
- Settings ▸ Import ▸ AI Denoise from switches it on for new raw photos shot at or above an ISO.
- A job over many photos (AI Denoise, exports) is one row in the activity stack with how many
  are done, the time left and the failures; its photos fold under it, and when it ends the app
  says once how it went.
