### Smooth grid scrolling with the Library Filter bar open
- With the Library Filter bar showing, the grid slowed down on large libraries: the bar listed every camera, lens and
  keyword on each frame, and went through every photo again after any change to the catalog. It now lists them only
  while one of its menus is open, and only again after a metadata change. On a 68,000-photo test library, scrolling
  with the bar open is as fast as with it hidden.
