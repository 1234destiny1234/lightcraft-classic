## Interface
- New **Modern – Simple** layout (Settings ▸ General ▸ Layout), replacing the touch layout: a photo-first Develop with a dock of adjustment groups and tools (bottom or side), cards of pill sliders, a ☰ menu bar with Shortcuts, and a floating histogram with two sizes. Classic is now called **Classic – Advanced**.
- **Pill** sliders (Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Sliders): the name and value inside one bar you drag anywhere.
