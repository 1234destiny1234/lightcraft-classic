### Folders panel laid out as in Lightroom Classic
- Each disk is a bar (status light, name, free space) with its folders nested below it; a click on the bar folds
  them. Before, the disk bars sat above everything and the folder tree started after the Local list, so the library's
  folders and the folders being browsed looked like one list.
- Local moved out of Folders into a Local Folders panel of its own at the bottom of the Library column, with a line
  saying its folders are shown in place, not in the catalog.
