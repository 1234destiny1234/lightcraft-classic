## Develop

- **High quality adjustment previews:** an optional setting (Settings ▸ Performance) keeps slider adjustments at the settled render's quality, size and display fit, including zoomed views, Before/After and HDR. When an edit is too slow for it, the moving slider falls back to the faster previews and the full quality comes back as soon as the slider holds still. The faster adaptive previews remain the default (contributed by Kaan88).
