### Smoother high quality adjustment previews

- With High quality adjustment previews on, slider drags in the fitted view update more often, and letting go of a slider still changes neither the picture nor the histogram, for HDR edits too.
- The fitted view settles sooner after every change: shrinking it to the size it is drawn takes a fraction of the time it did.
- Letting go of a slider on an HDR edit shown in HDR no longer flashes the histogram from before the drag.
