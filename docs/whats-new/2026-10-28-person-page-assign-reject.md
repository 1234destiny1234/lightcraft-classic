### Assign or reject several suggested faces at once on a person's page
- In a person's **More** suggestions, Ctrl/⌘-click a face, Shift-click for a range, or click the tick at its corner to select it; while some are selected a click adds or removes a face.
- A bar above the faces then assigns them all to someone else: typing offers the people already named, and **+ Create** makes a new person. Return assigns the typed name.
- **Reject** in the same bar leaves the selected faces out of People for good (they can be restored like other ignored faces). Each action is one undo step.
- Right-click a suggested face for **Assign to Someone Else…** or **Reject** on that face alone.
