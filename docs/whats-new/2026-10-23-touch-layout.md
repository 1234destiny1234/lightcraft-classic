### Touch layout on Android

- The photo fills the screen: tap it to show or hide the top bar and the tool strip (Auto, Presets, Basic, Colour, Tone, Detail, Optics, Effects, Crop, Masks, Remove).
- The slider tools adjust one setting at a time: Basic is white balance (Temp and Tint, in Kelvin for raw photos) and the tone sliders, Colour is texture, clarity, dehaze, vibrance and saturation, Tone is the parametric tone curve, Effects is vignette and grain.
- Drag sideways anywhere on the photo to change the setting, drag up or down to pick the next one, double-tap a setting's name to reset it, then Done or Cancel.
- Press and hold the photo, or the Before button, to see the original.
- All Edits opens the full Edit panel beside the photo in landscape and under it in portrait; tap the sheet's handle to close it.
- Library is the grid under a slim bar (Import ▸ Photos, Folder or Camera Roll; Filter; Grid Style; Settings); tap a photo to edit it.
- On the desktop, Settings ▸ General ▸ Layout (or Window ▸ Panels ▸ Touch Layout) turns the same layout on, for touch screens; it works with a mouse too, and the same Settings row switches back.
