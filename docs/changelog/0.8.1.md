# 0.8.1

## Fixes

- Import: a regression test covers rating, label, crop and develop settings from Lightroom-style `.xmp` sidecars beside raws (Add and Copy), and that a later folder sync never reverts your own edits. (#43) (no PR)
- The Develop tool strip shows only Develop's tools; Keywords and Info (Library's) open from the Window menu and their shortcuts (#45). (no PR)
- A mouse-wheel notch scrolls a third of the grid or filmstrip, as in Lightroom Classic, with a Wheel scroll
  speed preference (Normal / Fast / Fastest) under Preferences → Interface; port of MarkusFriedel's upstream
  change. (⇠ #1482)
- Faces: detection and recognition run their convolutions on the GPU when it gives the CPU's results and is faster
  (AuraFace about 2–2.5× faster); the CPU stays the reference and the fallback. (#35, ↑ #1663)
- The preset Amount slider fades the preset gradually down to 0 %: curves blend point by point, and the profile, treatment and switches take the preset's at any amount above 0 instead of snapping back at 50 %. (#37) (no PR)
- `cargo test -p lcc-ui-egui` runs the model-setup tests on its own: the crate's tests enable the engine's denoise
  feature instead of relying on the app or CLI to turn it on. (no PR)
