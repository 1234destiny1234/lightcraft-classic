### Preview for SDR Display
- With HDR editing on, a new Preview for SDR Display box under Visualize HDR shows how the photo looks on SDR screens and in SDR exports, so you can see what the SDR Rendition sliders do on an HDR display. It is the same switch as View ▸ HDR Preview.
- While the screen shows the photo in HDR, the SDR Rendition sliders are greyed out and say how to see them.
