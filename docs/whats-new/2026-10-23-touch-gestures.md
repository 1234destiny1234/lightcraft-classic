### Touch gestures, Back and screen edges

- Swipe to scroll the Library grid, the filmstrip, the tool strip and the panels; a quick swipe keeps gliding. Tap opens a photo; press and hold one to pick several (taps then add or remove), and drag after the hold to move them.
- Android's Back closes what is open, one step at a time: a dialog or menu, picking several photos, the adjust tool (keeping the change, as Done does), the Edit panel, then Develop back to the Library; at the Library it puts the app away.
- The top bar, the tool strip and the panels keep clear of the camera cutout, the status bar and the gesture bar.
- In adjust mode the setting being changed stays in the middle of its row.
- Develop's top bar has a Histogram button: pinned under the top bar in portrait, a small window you can drag anywhere in landscape (it stays where you put it).
