### Crop & Straighten and Transform as in Lightroom Classic
- The crop tool is Lightroom Classic's Crop & Straighten drawer: Aspect and its lock, Angle with the straighten tool
  and Auto, Constrain to Image, and Reset and Close. The Angle value takes a typed angle or a drag, so the separate
  angle field is gone.
- Upright, Constrain Crop and the transform sliders moved to a Transform section of the Edit panel, after Optics,
  with its own on/off switch. Rotate and flip are in the Photo menu and the guide overlays in View ▸ Tool Overlay,
  where Lightroom Classic keeps them.
