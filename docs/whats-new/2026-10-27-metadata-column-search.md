### Search the Metadata columns and the People view
- Each Metadata filter column now has a search box at its top: typing narrows that column's values as you type, a
  match in any case counts, and emptying the box brings every value back. The rest of the column list and the photos
  in the grid are untouched, and hiding the columns or the filter bar forgets what was typed.
- The People view has a search box too: it narrows the person cards to the names that hold what you type, and the
  unnamed faces stay below. The People button (or an emptied box) shows everyone again.
