### Touch layout on Android

- The photo fills the screen: tap it to show or hide the top bar and the tool strip (Auto, Presets, Light, Color, Effects, Detail, Optics, Crop, Masks, Remove).
- Light, Color, Effects, Detail and Optics adjust one setting at a time: drag sideways anywhere on the photo to change it, drag up or down to pick the next one, double-tap a setting's name to reset it, then Done or Cancel.
- Press and hold the photo, or the Before button, to see the original.
- All Edits opens the full Edit panel beside the photo in landscape and under it in portrait; tap the sheet's handle to close it.
- Library is the grid under a slim bar (Import ▸ Photos, Folder or Camera Roll; Filter; Grid Style; Settings); tap a photo to edit it.
- On the desktop, Window ▸ Panels ▸ Touch Layout turns the same layout on, for touch screens.
