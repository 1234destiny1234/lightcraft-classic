### The Grid's toolbar as in Lightroom Classic
- In the Library's Grid the toolbar has Lightroom Classic's items: Painter, Sort by name ("Sort: Capture Date"),
  colour labels after the rating and flags, Rotate Left and Right, Previous and Next, and the impromptu slideshow,
  next to the thumbnail size. A narrow window keeps the essentials, as before.
- Copy Edit Settings and its gear are gone from the Library's toolbar, as in Lightroom Classic. They stay in
  Develop's toolbar, and Photo ▸ Develop Settings copies and pastes in Library.
