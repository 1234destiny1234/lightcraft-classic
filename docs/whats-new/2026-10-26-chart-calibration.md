### Calibrate a camera with a colour chart
- The command-line tool's new `calibrate-chart` measures your camera's colour from raw photos of an X-Rite
  ColorChecker Classic, in daylight and optionally under tungsten. It shows the colour error of every patch before
  and after, writes a camera profile to try at once, and an entry that gives every photo from that camera model
  measured colour and Kelvin white balance.
