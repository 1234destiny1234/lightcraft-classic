## Colour

- **Eboda Colour profile:** a new Basic profile that gives a raw developed through a camera profile (`.dcp`, e.g. Adobe Standard) the look Lightroom Classic's default colour profile gives it. Fitted to Lightroom's export of a Leica SL photo, it brings the mean colour difference from 12.2 to 1.2 ΔE; only rendered output was observed.
- **Craft Colour:** the base profile (formerly "Color") is renamed; it renders as before.
- **Camera profiles keep their own tone:** a `.dcp` without a tone curve no longer takes the tone fitted to the camera's JPEG. With a Leica SL DNG and Adobe Standard, that fit desaturated midtones by about 15 %.
