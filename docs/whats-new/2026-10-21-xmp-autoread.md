### Edits from other apps are read automatically

- When DxO PhotoLab, ON1 or another app changes a photo's XMP sidecar and the photo hasn't changed here since the last sync, the app now reads the sidecar by itself, as one undoable step. When both sides changed, the conflict badge stays and you choose, as before.
- Turn it off in Settings ▸ XMP sidecars if you'd rather review each change.
