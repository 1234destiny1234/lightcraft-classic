### Faster HDR zoom and drags

- Zoomed in on an HDR edit, a pan keeps the part that was already sharp, and going back to a place you just looked at, or zooming out and in again, is sharp at once.
- HDR slider drags take less time per frame, and letting go of a slider at 1:1 settles much sooner.
- Settings ▸ Display & Speed ▸ Keep recent zoomed HDR views in graphics memory turns that memory off. It is offered only where the graphics card has room for it on your screen; elsewhere it is greyed out and says why.
