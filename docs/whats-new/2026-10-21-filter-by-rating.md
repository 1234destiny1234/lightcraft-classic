### Filter by Rating in the Library menu

- Library ▸ Filter by Rating sets the filter bar's rating without opening the bar: pick at least, exactly or at most, then the stars; picking the same stars again shows every photo. With one star and "at least", only rated photos stay in the grid, filmstrip and Select All, ready to review and export.
- Search takes `rating:>=1` and `rating:<=2` as well as `rating:>1` and `rating:3`.
