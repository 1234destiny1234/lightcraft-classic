### Tidier mask sliders, Color Grading inside masks
- A mask's sliders sit in sections you can fold: Light (with the Curve), Color, Color Grading, Effects and Detail. A dot beside a folded section's title says it changes something; folded sections stay folded.
- Color Grading inside a mask: pick Shadows, Midtones, Highlights or Global and set its Hue, Saturation and Luminance, as in the Color Grading panel, only where the mask is.
- Hovering a mask in the Masks list shows a bin beside its eye that deletes that mask.
