### Touch Settings and Export

- Settings in the touch layout is a full-screen page on a phone (categories, then each category as its own page) and a large sheet on a tablet, with finger-sized rows. It shows the same settings as the desktop dialog.
- Settings ▸ General ▸ Layout switches between the touch and the desktop layout, in either layout, and is remembered.
- Export from Develop's top bar or from a selection in Library: presets (full-size JPEG, 2048 px JPEG for sharing, 16-bit TIFF, DNG for raw photos), size and quality. On Android the files are saved to Pictures, or shared through the share sheet.
