### Reviewing faces with the scan switched off
- With a recognition model chosen, turning **Recognise faces** off no longer takes the review away: the **Unnamed faces** list still puts look-alikes together and still carries the name the named faces suggest, and a person's **More** faces are still listed, ready to confirm or hide (issue #685).
- The switch now only starts and stops looking for faces in new photos and learning them. Naming a face never needed it, and neither does being shown who the faces you already named look like.
