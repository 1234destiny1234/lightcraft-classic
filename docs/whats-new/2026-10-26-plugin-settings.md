### Plug-in settings
- A plug-in can have settings of its own: the Plug-in Manager shows a Settings… button on its row, which opens the
  plug-in's page, where you change them and click Save.
- A plug-in's dialogs open with the values you last ran them with.
- A plug-in can look at your selection before its dialog opens: it can offer choices that fit those photos, run at
  once when there is nothing to choose, or tell you why there is nothing to do.
